About the System
What are Phantom Units?

A transparent, rule-based framework for recognising every AXXSPACE contributor's work — with a real financial reference value behind each unit.

🏦
Fixed Unit Pool

There are exactly 1,000 Contribution Units in existence. Every unit awarded to a contributor comes from this fixed pool, ensuring scarcity and fairness.

💶
Real Monetary Value

Each unit carries a reference value of €10 (0.1 unit = €1). The full pool has a reference value of €10,000. This value is the basis for any future buy-back by AXXSPACE.

📊
Fractional Awards

Units are awarded in increments of 0.1, from minor tasks (0.1–0.2 units) to strategic contributions (0.9–1.0 units). Every award is logged with a task description, date, and reviewer.

🔒
Not Equity

Phantom Units are a recognition & reward mechanism only. They do not represent shares, equity, voting rights, or legal ownership in AXXSPACE (spec §9).

📋
Full Transparency

Every award is recorded on the contribution ledger with the task, reviewer, date and units. You can view your full history and track your growth over time.

🚀
Admin-Managed

All accounts and unit awards are managed by AXXSPACE admins — ensuring every award is properly reviewed, documented and reflects real contribution value.
